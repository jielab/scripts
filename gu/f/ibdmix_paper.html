Inputs and methods

  • Input and ranked window coordinates are 0-based, half-open. Intervals are merged per person and chromosome, including overlaps across references of the same lineage. Lineages can match the same region.
  • Coverage divides mean matched bases per person by the physical length of chromosomes with observed calls, including inaccessible sequence. Segment calls lack copy dosage.
  • The tested roster includes people with zero calls. Positive rows identify represented references. A chromosome with zero rows for a reference is excluded from its span because its tested status is unknown. Incomplete reference coverage is flagged.
  • Native calls are filtered by the recorded LOD and minimum-length thresholds, and start/end are converted to zero-based coordinates. No African Denisovan background subtraction is applied.
  • Denisovan calls measure matching to the named reference and can include ancestral polymorphism ; African Denisovan background subtraction is disabled.
  • Window scores divide total matched bases by window length and, when known, the tested sample count. The overlap count includes each person once per window. Adjacent windows can belong to the same locus.
  • Without a roster the report gives observed carriers and total coverage. The report contains aggregate results only.
