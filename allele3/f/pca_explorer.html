Source, original coordinates & interpretation

Source PC1/PC2 coordinates and recorded dates are unchanged; no PCA is fitted by this viewer. Color and size ranges are fixed over each complete supplied track. A time slice or group filter changes membership in the view, never the underlying coordinates or sample records. Optional bin midpoints and log1p spacing are display transformations only.

Supplied ALT copy number / dosage 0–2 describes one REF/ALT comparison; it is not three nucleotide states. Missing observations remain unavailable. Molecular, tolerance or selection labels are shown only when explicitly provided as sample-level features with provenance; the viewer does not estimate them. Visual separation and changing point density cannot establish natural selection.

Interaction design informed by Cell3D's multi-feature 2D/3D exploration. This atlas retains the project's predefined genetic coordinates and chronological axis.
